# QuantPilot

Stocks intelligence platform. LangGraph, Kafka, PySpark, FastAPI, PostgreSQL.

- Event-driven research platform for market events, technology trends, and second-order impact analysis.
- Kafka and PySpark pipelines for financial, news, and research data.
- Neo4j knowledge graph of companies, technologies, investments, and partnerships.
- Multi-agent research workflows with source verification, and PostgreSQL for traceable analysis.

Code: https://github.com/GuranshSinghKohli/quantpilot
