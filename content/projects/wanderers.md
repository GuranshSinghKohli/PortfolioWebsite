# Wanderers.space

Founding engineer. Waterloo. January 2026 to present. Prototype.

- Next.js campus social platform serving 46 monthly active verified University of Waterloo students.
- REST APIs, realtime pub/sub, and live chat for activity matching, feeds, and group interactions.
- Gemini, Google Maps, and K-means clustering for activity creation and personalized recommendations.
- Pitched at Velocity. Mentorship from 2 Velocity UW incubator mentors.
